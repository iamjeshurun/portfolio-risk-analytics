      How it is calculated

      Weights are the initial allocation of a buy-and-hold portfolio: nothing is rebalanced, so holdings drift with prices. Prices are daily adjusted closes (splits and dividends included). Only dates where every holding has a price are used; gaps inside that window are reported rather than filled. Return and volatility are annualized with 252 trading days per year, and Sharpe uses the risk-free rate you enter. Results describe the past sample only.
